## About

I’m **Rishi Bhor**, a Computer Science graduate specializing in **Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning**. I build data applications that connect business questions with structured analysis, practical automation, and clear interfaces.

My work brings a **software engineering mindset** to analytics: modular Python pipelines, SQL-backed validation, API boundaries, documented assumptions, and repeatable execution. I have built projects covering invoice processing, procurement forecasting, customer churn, and cloud-hosted AI assistants.

I’m developing **full stack application skills** through Streamlit interfaces, FastAPI services, databases, and Google Cloud deployments. My applied AI experience includes Gemini, Google ADK, tool-based retrieval, BigQuery integration, and human approval before operational changes.

I approach **product engineering** by starting with the user’s decision, building the smallest useful workflow, and making its outputs understandable. A forecast should help a buyer evaluate timing; an invoice check should explain why a payment needs review; an AI assistant should ground its response in available data.

**Open To**

Data Analyst · Business Intelligence Analyst · Reporting Analyst · MIS Executive · Junior Business Analyst · Entry-level opportunities in Python automation and applied AI
